# Ad Safety source register

Access date: 2026-08-25

This register separates external evidence from measured project outputs. Model-card and paper results describe the cited source. They do not establish performance on this project's dataset.

## Policy and taxonomy context

- Interactive Advertising Bureau, Ad Product Taxonomy: https://github.com/InteractiveAdvertisingBureau/Taxonomies/releases
- Google Ads policy overview: https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/6008942

These policies show why a visual cue is not always a final policy violation. Geography, audience, legality, and landing-page claims often require information outside the uploaded image.

## Pilot data

- ADautoGen-DS dataset card: https://huggingface.co/datasets/EthanGabis/ADautoGen-DS
- Weapons Set 1 dataset card: https://huggingface.co/datasets/rajshivanshuu/weapons_set1
- Wikimedia Commons API documentation: https://www.mediawiki.org/wiki/API:Imageinfo
- Wikimedia Commons licensing guidance: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Commons:Reusing_content_outside_Wikimedia

The financial-promotion examples generated by this project are synthetic. They test visible promotion cues only. They cannot prove fraud, regulatory status, or legality.

Listing a source records provenance, not redistribution permission. [DATASETS.md](DATASETS.md) is the canonical inventory and provenance record. [NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md) explains the reuse and license limits that apply to source material and course deliverables.
